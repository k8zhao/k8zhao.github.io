SCOPE

Contextual AI interactions in Edge

A custom Copilot component that works in any contextual scenario across Edge.

Overview

Designed to bring Copilot to users in context without opening the side panel. The component needed to work across any Edge surface, consumer and commercial, which meant designing something neutral and minimal enough to fit anywhere without feeling out of place. Starting with the context menu as the first test case, the pattern is now being adopted across three teams and multiple feature areas in Edge.
